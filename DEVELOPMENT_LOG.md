# Development Log

## 2026-06-06

### Session Summary

Thesisflow evolved from a VC article aggregator into an AI investment research
workspace. Four product phases were implemented locally on `production-prep`.
Nothing from this session was deployed or pushed automatically.

### Phase 1: AI Chat with Sources

- Added `/chat` and `POST /api/chat`.
- Added `article_chunks` with pgvector embeddings and full-text fallback.
- Existing articles were chunked and embedded in Neon.
- Answers use structured Chinese or English output based on the question language.
- Answers include inline citations and verified article source cards.
- Structured answer sections:
  - Core conclusion
  - Key insights
  - Why it matters
  - What to watch

### Phase 2: Investor View Comparison

- Added `/compare` and `POST /api/compare`.
- Supports 2-4 selected investors.
- Retrieval is filtered by the exact database source for each investor.
- Outputs core thesis, key arguments, agreements, disagreements, investment implications,
  and cited sources.
- Chinese topics generate Chinese analysis; English topics generate English analysis.

### Phase 3: Weekly Market Signal Dashboard

- Added `/weekly`.
- Added `weekly_market_reports` in Postgres.
- Replaced automatic category-level weekly summaries with one comprehensive report.
- Reports cover:
  - Top Signals This Week
  - Rising Topics
  - Investor Consensus
  - Investor Disagreements
  - Emerging Themes
- Worker generates the previous complete Monday-to-Sunday report on Mondays.
- A real report for `2026-06-01` through `2026-06-07` was generated from 32 articles,
  covering 4 investors and retaining 22 cited sources.

### Phase 4: Thesis Builder

- Added `/theses`, `/theses/new`, and `/theses/{id}`.
- Added Postgres tables `investment_theses` and `thesis_evidence`.
- Added signed anonymous workspace cookies with no user accounts.
- Supports article evidence and saved snapshots from Chat, Compare, and Weekly reports.
- AI develops:
  - Core claim
  - Supporting evidence
  - Counterarguments
  - Key questions
  - Investment implications
- Generates and stores an investment memo with deterministic verified Sources.
- Exports memo as Markdown.

### Risk Controls Implemented

- Every thesis query is scoped by signed anonymous `workspace_id`.
- Workspace cookie is HttpOnly and SameSite Lax.
- Maximum 20 evidence items per thesis.
- Maximum 25 theses per workspace.
- Article metadata is loaded server-side, not trusted from the browser.
- Imported research is marked as untrusted evidence in model prompts.
- Invalid AI evidence citations are removed server-side.
- Memo Sources are generated by the server from stored evidence.
- Imported URLs must use HTTP or HTTPS.
- Input, metadata, and evidence lengths are limited.
